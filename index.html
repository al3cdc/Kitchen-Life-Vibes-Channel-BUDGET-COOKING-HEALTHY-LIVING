Project Title

Kitchen Life Vibes Channel — Budget Cooking, Healthy Living (YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels)

Chosen Problem

Limited practical kitchen skills among Filipino teenagers and young adults (aged 13–25) significantly contribute to their heavy reliance on fast food and ultra-processed products.

Need Addressed

Helping beginners overcome low culinary confidence and excessive spending on fast food by teaching simple, accessible meal-prep skills on a tight budget. Our content addresses this need through short, non-intimidating videos featuring basic kitchen tools, minimal ingredients, and clear step-by-step instructions.

Relevance & Practicality Evaluation

Relevance: Learning to cook is a basic life skill that gives young people independence, saves money, and encourages healthier eating habits, steering them away from ultra-processed products and fast food deliveries.

Practicality: Recipes are realistic, utilizing everyday ingredients from local palengkes or sari-sari stores without requiring expensive appliances like air fryers or blenders.

Target Audience & Needs

Demographics: Students and young adults (ages 13–25) with minimal experience, busy daily schedules, and tight allowances who frequently rely on convenience foods.

Core Goal: To eat better, save money, and build kitchen confidence.

Specific Needs:

  • Quick meals that do not take hours to make.
  • Affordable recipes using common, low-cost ingredients.
  • Simple instructions that do not assume prior cooking knowledge.
  • Small portion sizes suitable for 1–2 people.
  • A friendly and encouraging tone that reduces the fear of making mistakes.
  • Clear health and nutrition information for those mindful of what they eat.

Research Findings & Reference Videos

A. YouTube Videos Analyzed

Video 1: Best 25 Simple Food Recipes to Make at Home — Easy Asian Cooking Collection by Hamimommy

Analysis: Adopts a calm, cozy, and aesthetic approach with soft, warm yellow lighting and smooth camera angle shifts (top-down, side views, macro close-ups). It features zero verbal commentary, relying entirely on gentle background music, natural kitchen ASMR sounds, and clear subtitles.

Video 2: Rice Cooker Meals on a Student Budget with Abi Marquez

Analysis: Features a professional, bright studio setup with a fixed face-to-camera angle enhanced by sudden zoom-ins on ingredients and actions. Driven by high-energy Taglish verbal commentary filled with personality and relatable student humor.

Video 3: 8 Cooking Recipes for College Students - Easy, Quick, and Affordable

Analysis: Uses a clean, clutter-free demonstration style focusing directly on the cutting board and cooking station. It relies on clean visual pacing, upbeat background music, and concise on-screen text outlining ingredients and steps without verbal speech.

Video 4: Meals So Easy A College Student Could Make It

Analysis: Features dynamic, fast-cut editing with expressive facial reactions, close-up food angles, and playful graphic pop-ups. It uses a humorous, conversational voiceover tailored to student culture.

B. Core Research Takeaways for Channel Strategy

  • Solve Real Problems: Target practical student challenges like limited budgets and minimal tools.
  • Know Your Audience: Match modern viewer preferences with snappy, mobile-optimized formats, and relatable Taglish communication.
  • Clear & Consistent Visuals: Combine appetizing close-up food shots with easy-to-read text overlays.
  • Build Genuine Connections: Keep the tone friendly, conversational, and non-judgmental to reduce kitchen intimidation.

Content Type & Specifications

Category: Short-form practical cooking videos.

Format & Platform: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels optimized for mobile viewing.

Duration: 45 to 120 seconds per clip.

Style: Fast-paced, step-by-step tutorials featuring budget-friendly, beginner-level recipes with vibrant text overlays, ASMR sound effects, and music.

Selection Justification

A. Solves a Real Need: Teaches essential cooking skills to those relying on expensive delivery.

B. Cheap & Easy to Produce: Uses common household tools and low-cost ingredients, keeping startup costs minimal for home filming.

C. Matches Modern Habits: Short, snappy formats appeal directly to TikTok and Reels audiences, making learning non-intimidating.

Project Content & Concept

A. Communication Style

Relatable & Conversational: Use modern, casual Taglish with student slang and humor about late-night cravings.

Encouraging & Non-Judgmental: Create a safe space for beginners by validating that cooking is intimidating.

B. Content Focus & Key Solutions

Budget-Friendly Dishes: Quick, delicious meals under ₱100–₱200 per person.

Kitchen Hacks: Simple shortcuts for meal prepping, cleaning, storage, and handling leftovers.

Technical & Skill Requirements

Technical Requirements

Equipment: Cameras, tripods, lighting gear (softboxes/ring lights), microphones, and reliable cloud storage.

Software: Video and audio editing tools like Adobe Premiere Pro, iMovie, Adobe Audition, and GarageBand.

Group Development Challenges & Solutions

Equipment & Pacing: Designing recipes for basic tools while keeping video pacing snappy and accurate to real-world cooking durations.

Mistakes & Leftovers: Demonstrating how to fix cooking errors live and repurposing extra ingredients to eliminate food waste.

Featured Student Budget Recipes

Flip through our simple, step-by-step pocket recipes designed for beginners and busy students!

1. Ginisang Upo with Sardines and Misua

A comforting, highly nourishing, and incredibly budget-friendly Filipino soup dish that stretches a single can of sardines into a hearty, savory meal for sharing.

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 15 mins Est. Cost: ₱70 – ₱90 Serving: 2–3 Persons

2. Tortang Talong with Giniling

A leveled-up twist on the classic Filipino eggplant omelet! Stuffed with savory minced pork (giniling) and coated in fluffy beaten eggs, this recipe packs extra protein and rich flavor on a student budget.

Prep Time: 15 mins Cook Time: 15 mins Est. Cost: ₱80 – ₱110 Serving: 2 Persons

3. Adobong Kangkong

A fast, healthy, and ultra-affordable classic Filipino vegetable dish! Fresh water spinach leaves and crunchy stems are sautéed in garlic, soy sauce, and vinegar, creating a savory-tangy meal that goes perfectly with warm rice.

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Est. Cost: ₱50 – ₱80 Serving: 2–3 Persons

4. Ensaladang Ampalaya with Egg

A nutritious and refreshing twist on bitter melon! Thinly sliced ampalaya is blanched or lightly sautéed with eggs, tomatoes, and onions, balancing out the bitterness with savory goodness. Perfect for health-conscious students.

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Est. Cost: ₱60 – ₱80 Serving: 2–3 Persons
